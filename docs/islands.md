# Islands

Use **Size** to filter the 24 playable islands. In portrait, open **Menu** to find the island picker. **Small** offers Kestrel Strait, The Narrows, The Basin, Reed Sound, Gullhook, Three Capes, and Foxridge. Foxridge has 85 land hexes along a winding ridge with a narrow bend between its northern and southern ends.
